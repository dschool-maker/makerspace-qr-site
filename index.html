How it works

Everything a new visitor needs to know before their first session — read this before you ask, it probably answers you already.

Membership

Access is membership-based. It costs nothing, but membership requires commitment — this is a community you join, not a facility you drop into once.

Open days

New visitors are encouraged to come to our open days: Tuesdays, 3pm–9pm. That's the easiest way to see the space, meet people, and figure out if it's for you.

Training & booking

Before using any machine, students need a short training session, arranged and booked through the training calendar. Once trained, machines are booked in advance the same way.

See booking calendars below.

Curriculum work

As a rule, we don't support using the makerspace for curriculum-based projects. If you need to do it for marks, do it at your own faculty with your own resources. If you want to explore something more meaningful than your coursework — that's exactly what this space is for.

Our values

Students are invited to explore making with these in mind, on every project.

Integrity

Make things honestly, and be honest about what you're making and why.

Mastery

Make things really well, continually looking for ways to improve your craft.

Sustainability

Make things in a way that's environmentally, societally and financially sustainable.

Collaboration

Make things with your friends — new and old. Find new and interesting people to work with.

What to make

Projects that explore innovation, creativity and practical problem solving are encouraged.

Good directions

  • Things that stimulate thinking or conversation
  • Solutions to social or environmental problems
  • Starting new businesses
  • Or just fixing a broken gear lever on your bicycle

Avoid

  • Replicating meaningless objects in a new form of consumerism
  • Making things purely because the machine can
Make things that are good for people and the planet — that's the filter for every project here.

Join us if you want to work with people that

  • › Leave things better than they found them.
  • › Give a little bit more than they take.
  • › Seek to learn, share, inspire and teach.
  • › Are respectful of people, the environment and equipment.
