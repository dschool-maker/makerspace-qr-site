Cutting Machine

Cricut Maker 4

A precision cutting machine for vinyl, paper, cardstock, iron-on and fabric. One of the friendliest machines in the space for total beginners.

Idea → finished product

  1. 1

    Design in Design Space. Cricut's free software (desktop, web or mobile). Use their templates and images, or upload your own SVG/image.

  2. 2

    Pick your material. Choose vinyl, iron-on, cardstock, or fabric — Design Space auto-suggests blade and pressure settings per material.

  3. 3

    Load the mat (or go matless). Smart Materials can feed straight in without a mat; most vinyl/cardstock needs a mat.

  4. 4

    Cut, then weed. "Weeding" means peeling away the negative space around your design with a weeding tool — that's the fiddly bit.

  5. 5

    Apply. Transfer tape for vinyl decals, heat/iron for iron-on. Then it's done.

Quickstart

  • 1

    Open Design Space and sign in (create a free account if you don't have one).

  • 2

    Connect the Maker 4 via USB or Bluetooth (first time: Menu → New Product Setup).

  • 3

    Build or open your design on the canvas, then click "Make It".

  • 4

    Select your material from the material list — this sets blade depth and cut pressure for you.

  • 5

    Load your mat/material and press the flashing "Go" button on the machine.

⚠ Safety & who to ask

  • Blades are sharp — handle by the housing, not the tip, and store them capped when not in use.
  • Keep fingers clear of the carriage track while a cut is running.
  • Use a cutting mat for anything other than Smart Materials, or you risk cutting into the machine itself.
  • Return blades and tools to their labeled spots when you're done — the next person needs them too.

New to cutting machines entirely? Ask staff for a two-minute walkthrough before your first project.
