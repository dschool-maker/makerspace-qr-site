Sewing & Embroidery

Bernette b05 Academy sewing machine and Bernette b79 embroidery machine — general stitching plus machine embroidery.

Idea → finished product

  1. 1

    Plan your piece. Sketch it, take measurements, and cut your fabric pieces with seam allowance included.

  2. 2

    Thread up and test. Wind a bobbin, thread the machine, and always run a test seam on scrap fabric first to check tension and stitch.

  3. 3

    Sew your seams on the b05, using the right stitch and presser foot for your fabric and seam type.

  4. 4

    Add embroidery (optional). Hoop your fabric with stabilizer, load your design onto the b79 embroidery module, and let it stitch it out.

  5. 5

    Finish. Trim loose threads, press seams, and it's done.

Quickstart — b05 Academy (sewing)

  • 1

    Wind a bobbin and load it into the bobbin case.

  • 2

    Thread the top of the machine following the numbered thread path, using the built-in needle threader for the eye of the needle.

  • 3

    Choose your stitch (straight stitch is the default for most seams) and set stitch length/width.

  • 4

    Test on a scrap of your actual fabric and check tension before sewing your real seam.

  • 5

    Sew slowly at first — use the speed limiter if you're new to the machine.

Quickstart — b79 (embroidery)

  • 1

    Attach the embroidery module to the machine.

  • 2

    Hoop your fabric with the correct stabilizer backing for your fabric weight.

  • 3

    Load your embroidery design (built-in designs, or your own file via USB) on the touchscreen.

  • 4

    Attach the hoop to the module and let the machine's positioning guide confirm placement.

  • 5

    Start the design and keep an eye on thread breaks or color changes that need a manual thread swap.

⚠ Safety & who to ask

  • Turn the power switch to "0" before rethreading, changing needles, or changing the presser foot.
  • Keep fingers away from the needle area while sewing — never push or pull the fabric.
  • Never leave either machine plugged in and unattended.
  • Use the correct bernette stitch plate — a mismatched one can cause needle breakage.
  • Don't use bent or damaged needles.

First time embroidering? Ask staff for a hooping and stabilizer walkthrough before your first run.
