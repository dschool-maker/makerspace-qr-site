Workbench

Hand Tools

Screwdrivers, clamps, hand saws, files, drills, hammers and the rest of the general workbench toolkit.

Idea → finished product

  1. 1

    Plan the build. Sketch what you're making and list the steps — cutting, drilling, joining, finishing.

  2. 2

    Measure and mark before you cut or drill anything. "Measure twice, cut once" exists for a reason.

  3. 3

    Work through it a step at a time — clamp your piece securely before cutting or drilling into it.

  4. 4

    Clean up and return every tool to its spot when you're done, whether you finished the project or not.

Quickstart

  • 1

    Check the tool board / drawer to see what's available and where it lives.

  • 2

    Inspect a tool before using it — loose heads, frayed cords, or dull blades should be flagged, not used.

  • 3

    Secure your workpiece (clamp or vise) rather than holding it in your hand while cutting or drilling.

  • 4

    Use the right tool for the job rather than forcing one to do something it isn't meant for.

  • 5

    Clean, dry, and return each tool to its labeled spot after use.

⚠ Safety & who to ask

  • Safety glasses on for any cutting, drilling, or hammering.
  • Never carry sharp or pointed tools with the tip facing someone.
  • Clamp your work down — don't hold small pieces by hand near a blade or drill bit.
  • If a power tool is involved (drill, jigsaw, etc.), get a quick check-out from staff first if you haven't used that exact tool here before.
  • Report any damaged or missing tools rather than leaving them for the next person to discover.

Not sure which tool fits the job? Ask a makerspace staff member — that's what they're there for.
